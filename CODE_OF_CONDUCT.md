# Community code of conduct

Merhatta Softwares is committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free project community.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, protect private information, and focus discussion on improving the product. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, spam, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security reporting channel.
